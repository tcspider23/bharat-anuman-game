How It Works

Step-by-step flow of the game — from thinking to guessing!
1
Think of any Indian personality
2
Select a game mode (Cricket, Bollywood, etc.)
3
Detective Anuman asks smart questions
4
Answer: YES / NO / PROBABLY / DON'T KNOW
5
AI updates probabilities with each answer
6
Final guess when AI is confident!
